      Privacy
      
Messages are actually encrypted.

      
End-to-end. I didn’t add a lock icon for the screenshot. Here’s what’s private, what isn’t, and what’s still early.

    


    

      

        
Encrypted DMs

        
The server can’t read message bodies as plain text. Optional key backup stays under your control.

      

      

        
What the AI can see

        
Whatever you type, the thread you’re in, and (in agent mode) what’s on screen. Don’t put secrets in AI chat you wouldn’t give any other model.

      

      

        
Public posts stay public

        
If you publish it, people can see it. That’s the product. You can also make your account private so only the people you allow see your profile and posts.

      

      

        
Still early

        
~69 users. One builder. The agent can still make things up; the verify step reduces that, it doesn’t erase it.

      The build
      
No team showed up.
So the AI became the extra
pair of hands.

      
I built Glowwww alone, as a college student, from scratch, over five to six months. No funding. No team. I was tired of starting side projects — I wanted one piece of infrastructure I could keep adding to.

      
That’s why the AI isn’t a chatbot tab. It’s agentic, inside the app. 𝕏 and Instagram still don’t really use AI for social. I did. Late nights, failed experiments, rewrites, and whatever the first ~69 people told me was broken.
